## Is it serious?

For most people, **no**. It is a minor skin problem that comes and goes. The worry around it is much bigger than the real harm.

## Can it be treated?

There is no cure, but medicine can stop or shorten outbreaks and lower the chance of passing it to someone else.

## Good to know

A normal STI test usually does **not** check for herpes unless you have a sore. If you want this test, ask for it.
