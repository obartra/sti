## Would I know if I had it?

Often there are no signs. Many people feel fine and still have it. A **test** is the only way to be sure.

## Can it be cured?

**Yes.** One shot of antibiotic medicine cures it.

## What should I do?

Get a test. If you have it, get the medicine. Tell people you had sex with lately, so they can get tested too.
